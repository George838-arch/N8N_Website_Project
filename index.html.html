The 3 Engineered n8n Workflows

Direct representation of the workflows powering this recruitment automation system.

Workflow 1 · Main Entry Point

CV Recruiter Intake

The ingestion gateway. Triggered via n8n Form submission, validates candidate name and @gmail.com email, queries Google Sheets to prevent duplicate applications for the same role, names and uploads the CV PDF to Google Drive ('MEDI Company'), and queues the candidate with status 'NEW'.

Form Trigger Google Drive API Google Sheets Input Validation
Workflow 2 · Autonomous AI Agent

Recruiter Check AI Agent

The evaluation brain. Triggered daily at 9:00 AM or via manual execution. Fetches queued candidates, downloads the CV from Google Drive, routes to native text extraction or Gemini 3.5 Vision OCR, extracts structured data, applies the 100-point rubric, and emails candidate & HR.

Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite Vision OCR Fallback Information Extractor Gmail OAuth2
Workflow 3 · Resiliency & SLA

Error AI Recruiter

Centralized error and SLA monitor. Catches failures from Workflow 1 and 2, classifies root causes (Auth, Rate Limits, AI Format, Data), throttles alerts to 1/hour per fingerprint, sends an 8:00 AM daily digest, and flags candidates stuck over 2 hours.

Error Trigger Hook Alert Throttling SLA Watchdog (>2h) Daily Digests
